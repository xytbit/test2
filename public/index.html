01 — What is DCITC

We are the people who ask how, then why.

DCITC is the technology club of Dhaka College. We are not a lecture series and not a fan club for frameworks. We are a student-run study organization: fundamentals first, projects second, trends never.

Identity

A room where someone can spend a Tuesday evening tracing a TCP handshake with tcpdump, and a Thursday one debugging a Bengali stemmer with the people who wrote it.

02 — Philosophy

Three verbs, one operating principle.

Everything the club does is one of these three actions. If an activity is none of them, it does not survive.

01 / INPUT

Curate

We read the documentation, pick the resources that survive scrutiny, and keep the noise out. Good material is a public good; we maintain it.

02 / PROCESS

Build

Reading without building is collecting. Every study group ends in a project: a tool, a measurement, a mirror, a paper trace.

03 / OUTPUT

Understand

Trends come and go. Fundamentals compound. We trade the impressive for the durable and explain everything until it is obvious.

03 — Sectors of Our Club

Explore Our Departments.

01 Administration Weekly, public, notes-first. Systems, algorithms, AI/ML, web, security, electronics.
02 Publications Real problems, shipped. From a Bengali NLP toolkit to lab dataloggers — all documented.
03 Programming and Robotics Hands-on, small, and repeatable. Terminal, Git, networking, measurement — not slide decks.
04 Web Development and Cyber Security We write down what we learn. Kernel paths, eBPF, field reports — the club's public memory.
05 Interactive Media and Automation We host our own mirrors, journal, and CTF infra. Running things teaches more than any tutorial.
