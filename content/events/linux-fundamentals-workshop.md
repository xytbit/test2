Bring a machine you can break. We hand out lab accounts at the door, so there is nothing to install.

## What to expect

- one mentor per table, live systems from minute one,
- the fix-the-broken-box challenge at the end — a config we sabotaged ourselves,
- no slides; everything happens in a terminal projected big.

If you already live in a shell, come anyway: the challenge is harder than it looks.
