01 — What is DCITC

We are the people who ask how, then why.

The first stage of learning is to understand how something works, and then ask why it works. From there comes the real question: what changes could make it better, worse, or entirely different. That kind of thinking is what a genuinely passionate technologist should have.

Identity

This is a place for people who talk about technology, work with it, and stay curious about where it is heading. Some come to build, some come to understand, and some come simply to ask questions no one else is asking. What holds the group together is not a shared skill level, but a shared interest in taking technology seriously.

02 — Philosophy

Three verbs, one operating principle.

This is a place for people who talk about technology, work with it, and stay curious about where it is heading.

01 / INPUT

Curate

Every project starts with an idea that has not been tested against reality yet. Turning that idea into something real, even a rough version, is what separates thinking about a problem from actually learning from it.

02 / PROCESS

Build

An idea only proves itself once it takes a working form. Building forces decisions that thinking alone never demands, and those decisions are where most of the real learning happens.

03 / OUTPUT

Understand

Finishing something is not the same as knowing why it works. Going back to examine the reasoning underneath a finished project turns a one time success into knowledge that carries forward to the next one.
